# CO2 AI

> CO2 AI is an enterprise sustainability platform that helps organizations measure, reduce, and report carbon emissions across their entire value chain, combining trustworthy emissions data, AI-powered workflows, and supply-chain collaboration. Backed by Partech. No public developer API is published as of this writing.

## Company
- [Website](https://co2ai.com/): CO2 AI AI-powered sustainability action platform
- [Corporate Footprinting](https://co2ai.com/corporate-footprinting): Scope 1/2/3 corporate carbon footprinting
- [Decarbonization](https://co2ai.com/decarbonization): Reduction planning and Marginal Abatement Cost analysis
- [Request a demo](https://co2ai.com/request-a-demo): Sign up / contact sales
